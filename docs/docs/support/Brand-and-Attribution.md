## Usage boundary

- Diagrams and architecture illustrations in this site are original RevitDevTool SVG/Mermaid artwork.
- Third-party logos remain unchanged and are displayed smaller than the RevitDevTool identity.
- Do not interpret these references as an affiliation, certification, or endorsement.
- Software licenses for a project do not automatically grant ownership of its trademarks or logos. Follow the relevant brand guidance when reusing these assets outside this documentation site.

For Microsoft marks, consult the [Microsoft Trademark and Brand Guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks). For Autodesk marks, consult the [Autodesk trademark guidelines](https://www.autodesk.com/company/legal-notices-trademarks/trademarks/guidelines-for-use).
